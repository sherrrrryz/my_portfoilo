## Why Canada
After COVID, a lot changed for me -- like it did for many people. I realized that instead of staying in one place and going deeper, what mattered more to me was to see more of the world, experience different ways of living, and try things I'd never tried before. I believe North America is at the center of tech innovation, so between Europe and other regions, I chose here. And between the US and Canada -- with everything going on in the US -- Canada felt like the right call.

## Why I Left AppLovin
AppLovin is a really strong company, and I had great colleagues and leaders there. I'm genuinely grateful that they supported my relocation from China to Canada -- that move was important to me both financially and personally. But the company was going through a lot of rapid changes, and at the same time I realized my English still needed serious work. I decided to step back, invest in my language skills, and prepare properly for the next chapter in North America.
